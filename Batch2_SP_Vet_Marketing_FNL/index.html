Pets are an important part of the family. That’s why we create high-quality nutritional support for your dogs, cats, and horses all with the same purity, safety, manufacturing, and quality standards used for our Standard Process human supplements. Our mission is to change the lives of families and their animals with targeted, whole food-based nutritional support. Our veterinary supplements empower health care professionals and pet owners to care for their animals in the same way they care for themselves. We believe that, given the proper nutrition, we have the amazing capability to impact our pets’ health.

Individualized Care for Dogs, Cats, and Horses

Dogs, cats, and horses have individual nutritional needs beyond what their regular diets provide. These unique needs change based on age, activity level, and health status.
