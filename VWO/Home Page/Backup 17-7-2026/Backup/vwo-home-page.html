Redefining whole food nutrition

The Nutrition Innovation Center applies systems biology and translational science to examine food synergy within whole food-based supplements as integrated systems.

Located at the North Carolina Research Campus, our multidisciplinary team connects soil conditions, nutrient complexity, formulation design, and physiological outcomes — informing product decisions with measurable insight.
