Healthspan Revolution Holistic Strategies for Aging Well

Whole food-based nutrition, herbs, and lifestyle interventions are foundational to this approach. They work together to reduce inflammation, enhance mitochondrial function, and support the resilience of the neuroendocrine and immune systems—key pillars of aging well. Health professionals who integrate these tools into their practice are uniquely equipped to lead a healthspan revolution, empowering patients to live longer, healthier, and more independent lives.

SPEAKERS

Deanna Minich MS, PhD, CNS, IFMCP

Globally recognized nutrition scientist, author, and functional medicine leader. With 20+ years in clinical research and supplement innovation, she bridges science, soul, and systems-based healing through her Food & Spirit™ publications.

SESSION TOPIC | Healthspan Revolution: Whole Food Nutrition as a Clinical Strategy for Aging Well

This keynote session presents whole food-based nutrition as a cornerstone of clinical strategies to extend healthspan and vitality. Participants will examine the science linking dietary patterns and phytonutrients to healthy aging and learn to apply color-coded, food-based frameworks that translate complex nutritional concepts into personalized, actionable plans for patients.

DAVE HOGSED DOM, AP

A clinician at Natural Healthcare Professionals in Fort Myers, Florida, specializing in nutritional support for endocrine, digestive, musculoskeletal, and immune health. A consultant and speaker for Standard Process since 2003, he is recognized for simplifying clinical nutrition and has taught post-graduate programs at leading chiropractic colleges nationwide.

SESSION TOPIC | The Rainbow Prescription: Correcting Dietary Color Deficiencies for Optimal Aging

This session highlights the clinical importance of dietary color diversity as a marker of phytonutrient adequacy and healthy aging. Participants will learn to recognize signs of “color deficiencies” in the diet and apply a targeted, color-based nutrition strategy to optimize antioxidant defenses, cognitive vitality, and cardiometabolic resilience.

SIMON AGGER DC

A chiropractor and clinical nutritionist based in Portland, OR. He serves as attending physician for the Oregon ballet theater and mentors future practitioners at UWS. Dr. Agger lectures on natural health, emphasizing innovative, evidence-informed approaches to both common and complex health challenges.

SESSION TOPIC | The Endothelial Glycocalyx: A Missing Link in both Cardiovascular Risk & Cognitive Resilience

This session explores the critical yet often overlooked role of the endothelial glycocalyx in vascular and cognitive health. Participants will gain insights into how metabolic stressors degrade this protective barrier and discover nutritional and botanical strategies to restore endothelial integrity, support blood-brain barrier function, and reduce cardiometabolic risk.

ALAN BARRIER NTP

A Product Sales Trainer at Standard Process, Inc., where he leverages over 17 years of experience helping integrative and alternative health care practitioners implement whole food-based nutritional strategies. Drawing from a deep understanding of human physiology, clinical nutrition, and behavioral influence, Alan empowers professionals to bridge the gap between science and practical patient outcomes.

PANEL | Product Insights and Clinical Application

Join us for a moderated Q&A panel where experts discuss key product insights, including formulation, function, and clinical relevance. Panelists will share real-world experiences and practical approaches for integrating these products into patient care to enhance outcomes.

MIMI HERNANDEZ MS, RH (AHG)

A registered herbalist and former executive director of the American Herbalist Guild. With decades of experience in ethnobotany and herbal education, she has taught at Frostburg State University and authored National Geographic Herbal. In 2023, she received the American Botanical Council’s Botanical Excellence Award.

SESSION TOPIC | Turning Down the Heat: Clinical Perspectives on Age-Related Inflammation

This talk explores the clinical phenomenon of “inflammaging,” examining how chronic low-grade inflammation contributes to age-related functional decline. Participants will gain practical strategies to modulate inflammatory pathways through nutrition, botanicals, and lifestyle interventions that support resilience and healthy aging.

LOWELL KEPPEL DC

A chiropractor practicing in Westminster, Colorado for over 30 years, integrating nutrition, acupuncture, and emotional health into his practice. A certified practitioner of Neuro Emotional Technique and herbal therapy, he teaches nationally on Standard Process and MediHerb, sharing his expertise in whole food nutrition and functional approaches to patient care.

SESSION TOPIC | From Restless Nights to Restorative Sleep: Supporting Circadian Rhythms and Sleep Architecture for Healthy Aging

This session explores how aging affects circadian rhythms and sleep architecture, leading to declines in metabolic, cognitive, and emotional health. Participants will learn to identify key disruptors of restorative sleep and implement nutrition and lifestyle interventions that realign circadian biology and support healthy aging.

SARAH KOTLERMAN BS, DC, NTP

Clinical lead at a Averio Health Institute, specializing in regenerative chiropractic care for patients with chronic conditions. A board member of the Washington State Chiropractic Association, she is passionate about reversing spinal ligament instability and advancing chiropractic education through research, leadership, and college development initiatives.

SESSION TOPIC | Brain Aging and Neuroprotection: Clinical Strategies for Cognitive Preservation and Recovery

This session delves into the mechanisms of brain aging and the causes of cognitive decline. Participants will gain practical tools to identify early signs of neurodegeneration and implement integrative, evidence-based strategies to preserve cognitive function and promote neurological resilience.

DAINA PARENT ND

Clinical educator at Standard Process and licensed Naturopathic Physician, specializing in aesthetics, anti-aging, and primary care with a focus on hormone balance, gut, and thyroid support. Dr. Parent empowers practitioners and patients through evidence-based integrative medicine.

SESSION TOPIC | When More Isn’t Better: Confronting the Hazards of Polypharmacy

This talk addresses the growing challenge of polypharmacy in aging populations, highlighting its contribution to cognitive decline, frailty, and nutrient depletion. Participants will explore strategies for identifying harmful drug interactions, implementing deprescribing approaches, and integrating nutritional and botanical alternatives to minimize medication burden and enhance patient outcomes.

DELLA PARKER ND

Naturopathic Physician and owner of Stellar Health and Wellness, PC. With expertise in applied kinesiology, nutritional counseling, and functional lab evaluation, Dr. Parker integrates scientific and holistic approaches to address root causes of imbalance and promote optimal health, energy, and resilience for her patients.

SESSION TOPIC | GLP-1 Agonists: Natural Alternatives and Nutritional Support for Pre- and Post-Intervention

This session explores the evolving role of GLP-1 receptor agonists in managing obesity and type 2 diabetes, with a focus on how nutrition support and patient-centered interventions can enhance therapeutic outcomes while mitigating potential side effects. The presentation will provide evidence-based insights into the mechanisms and benefits of GLP-1 agonists, strategies for nutritional optimization, and practical approaches to behavioral and psychosocial patient support.

Jessica Stefanski NMD, LAc, FABNE

Jessica Stefanski NMD LAc FABNE is the Clinical Education Manager at Standard Process. She is a licensed naturopathic physician, a licensed acupuncturist, and is board-certified in naturopathic endocrinology. Her clinical work has focused on the integrative treatment of women’s health and hormone conditions.

SESSION TOPIC | Gut Resilience in Aging: Supporting the Microbiome for Longevity and Vitality

This session explores how aging affects the gut microbiome and its profound impact on immunity, metabolism, and inflammation. Participants will learn evidence-based approaches using nutrition, phytonutrients, probiotics, and lifestyle strategies to restore microbial diversity, strengthen gut integrity, and promote healthy longevity.
