Getting started is easy. First, create your Standard Process user account and verify your email address. Once your email has been confirmed, you can connect with a Healthcare Practitioner in multiple ways:

How Patient Direct Works

Patient Direct makes it easy to build and maintain the connections.

There are three easy ways to connect:

  • Healthcare Practitioner Invitation – Healthcare Practitioners can invite patients directly to join their practice through Patient Direct.
  • Patient Request – Patients can create an account and request to become a patient of any participating Healthcare Practitioner.
  • Healthcare Practitioner Code – Patients can enter a unique code provided by their Healthcare Practitioner to connect directly with their practice.

Once connected, Patient Direct creates a seamless experience that keeps personalized care and recommended nutrition in one place. Healthcare Practitioners can develop individualized or reusable supplement protocols, while patients can view their recommendations and purchase products online anytime.
