Get Started with Patient Direct

Patient Direct is designed to help you extend your care beyond the office while making it easier for patients to access the products and guidance you recommend. By completing a few simple setup steps, you can increase your practice visibility, create personalized wellness plans, strengthen patient engagement, and customize the purchasing experience to align with your goals.

Together, these tools help you deliver more personalized care, improve patient follow-through, and build stronger relationships throughout the wellness journey.

What's next

Start by verifying your practice locations. Your default business address is already listed; simply make any updates and add additional office locations so patients can find you through the Standard Process® Where to Buy page. Then create protocols to organize your recommendations into clear, actionable care plans. Invite patients to connect with your practice online, enabling ongoing support and easier access to resources. Finally, configure your Patient Direct pricing and AutoShip options to create a seamless experience that supports both patient adherence and practice growth.

Step 1: Set Up Practice Location

Manage Your Practice Locations

Make it easy for patients to find and connect with your practice. By adding and maintaining your practice locations, you'll increase your visibility on the Standard Process® Where to Buy page and help patients discover the products and professional support they're looking for.

Whether you have multiple offices or are expanding your practice, keeping your locations up to date ensures patients can find the most convenient option for receiving personalized care.

Practice locations

Additional Locations?

1. Go to Practice Locations

2. Click + Create Location

3. Add required info and click Save

Why is this important?

Being listed on the Where to Buy page puts your practice in front of patients who are already looking for Standard Process products—and the professional guidance to use them effectively. It's a simple way to increase visibility, attract new patients, and grow your business while reinforcing your role as a trusted provider of personalized care.

Step 2: Create Protocols

Create and Share Personalized Protocols

Protocols make it easy to turn your recommendations into a clear, actionable plan for your patients. By combining products, quantities, and personalized guidance into a single protocol, you can streamline follow-through, improve the patient experience, and help patients stay on track with your recommendations.

Protocols provide a simple, organized way to deliver personalized support at scale. They give individuals greater clarity around their next steps while helping you communicate care plans more efficiently and consistently.

Create a Protocol:

1. Go to Protocols

2. Click Create Protocol

3. Name the Protocol

4. Add a Description

5. Add Notes for the Patient

6. Add the product and quantity

7. Click Create

Step 3: Invite Patients

Invite and Connect with Patients

Inviting patients allows you to extend your care beyond the office and create a more connected experience. Once connected, you can share personalized protocols, provide ongoing support, and help patients access the products and resources they need to stay engaged in their wellness journey.

This creates a direct connection between your practice and their online experience. It enables more personalized support, strengthens engagement, and gives you greater visibility into how patients are interacting with your recommendations.

Invite Patients:

1. Go to Manage Patients

2. Click Invite Patient

3. Add Email, First & Last Name of New or Existing Users

4. Click Send Invitation

Once the Patient has logged in and accepted the invitation you can begin managing their care—view details, share protocols, and track orders.

Step 4: Patient Direct Pricing

Configure Your Patient Direct Pricing

Patient Direct Pricing gives you flexibility in how products are offered to your patients. Customize pricing and AutoShip settings to align with your practice goals, create a consistent patient experience, and encourage long-term adherence to wellness plans.

Thoughtful pricing settings can help improve patient retention, encourage ongoing product use, and support long-term wellness outcomes. By tailoring these options to your practice, you can create an experience that benefits both your patients and your business.

Patient Direct Pricing

1. Go to Patient Direct Pricing

2. Enable or Disable AutoShip Discount

3. Select Pricing Options

4. Click Save
