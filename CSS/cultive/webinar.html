The Pharmacist’s Guide to Modern Menopause

Tuesday, November 17, 2026 6:00 PM - 7:00 PM CT

Non-CE

On-line Webinar

Menopause is more than declining estrogen levels — it’s a complex metabolic and physiologic transition affecting the brain, thyroid, adrenal function, and long-term health outcomes. Join Lark Swofford, RPh, and Veronica Riera-Gilley, PharmD, as they share their clinical experience working with women from perimenopause through postmenopause.
