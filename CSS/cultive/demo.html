Who qualifies?

Applications are approved or pending for:

  • Acupuncture/Oriental Medicine (CA Board, NCBAHM)
  • Chiropractic (Northeast College of Health Sciences)
    • American Clinical Board of Nutrition (ACBN)
  • Board Certified Holistic Nutrition (NANP)
  • Naturopathic Physicians (AANP)
    • Nursing (CA Board of Registered Nursing)
  • Board for Certification of Nutrition Specialists (BCNS)

*Note: Nutritionists certified through the Nutritional Therapy Association (NTA) no longer require CEUs for licensure.

How do I register for CEUs?

When registering for Cultivate 2027, select the location and “CEU Included” option and provide the following:

  • Full name
  • Credentials
  • Mailing address and/or business address
  • Phone number
  • State(s) where you practice
  • License or certificate number (for each state you practice in and are requesting CEUs)

Already registered? You can modify your registration from your confirmation email or contact: events@standardprocess.com

What does the CEU fee cover?

The CEU fee that was paid along with your registration covers the application fees for each practitioner type and in some cases, fees for each state accepting our CEU application. If your state or license board does not accept our application to offer CEUs, you will be notified via email and the difference in rate (non-CEU vs. CEU) will be refunded within 30 days of the event.

Are there any other requirements to earn CEUs?

Yes, our co-sponsors require:

  • All attendees must complete a conference feedback survey. The survey will be sent via email shortly after the event.
  • Attendees will only be eligible for the number of CEU hours based on their time in attendance.
  • All CEU attendees are required to check in and out of the session room each day. Please ensure you visit the designated check-in station each time you enter or leave the seminar room.
How do I receive my CEU certificate?

CEU certificates are emailed to practitioners. Please check your email spam or junk folder starting at 3 weeks post-event. If you do not receive your certificate within 30 days of the event, please contact ce@standardprocess.com.

Can I earn CEUs for more than one state? Or more than one profession?

Yes, you can earn CEUs toward multiple state licenses (in the case of our Acupuncture or Chiropractic practitioners). When completing the registration, indicate each credential for which you are CEU eligible, plus the license or certificate number associated with each profession. For example, Acupuncture certificate #12345 and Chiropractic in Alabama #CHI 3456. For those attendees requesting CEUs for multiple professions or multiple states, you’ll pay only one CEU fee.

I forgot to register for my CEUs, can I still earn them on-site?

You will need to speak to one of our Standard Process staff members at the event to get registered for CEUs and complete the sign-in process for each morning and afternoon session.

*We are unable to issue CEUs for in-person attendees who decide AFTER the event that they would have liked to earn CEUs.

I don’t see my profession on the approved list of CEUs, can I still earn them?

Unfortunately, no. We can only issue CEUs for credentials for which we’ve received approval. We can provide a certificate of attendance that can be submitted to your licensing board. Ultimately, it is up to your licensing board to consider awarding credit for the event.

Who do I contact if I have a CEU-related question before, during, or after the symposium?

Please reach out to our Support Team at ce@standardprocess.com.
