MediHerb® GLP-1 Bitter Complex: A Botanical Approach to Satiety Signaling

Advances in GLP-1 research have transformed practitioners' understanding of appetite regulation, satiety signaling, and digestive physiology. Emerging evidence suggests that bitter botanical compounds, acting through taste receptor–mediated mechanisms in the gut, may help support the body's natural secretion of satiety-regulating hormones. Join Clinical Educator Mimi Hernandez, MS, RH(AHG) for an exclusive first look at GLP-1 Bitter Complex, a new formula from MediHerb® featuring standardized hops and feverfew extracts. This session will explore the science behind the formula, its traditional herbal foundations, and practical strategies for integrating it into clinical protocols that support healthy satiety, digestive function, and endogenous GLP-1 physiology.

Mimi Hernandez MS, RH (AHG)

Mimi Prunella Hernandez, MS, RH (AHG), has been a Registered Herbalist with the American Herbalists Guild since 2002. She graduated from Maryland University of Integrative Health, where she completed clinical and research tracks. For over a decade, Mimi served as the executive director of the American Herbalists Guild, where she worked tirelessly to ensure that clinical herbalists have a solid professional community to support their work. Before this, she taught undergraduate ethnobotany at Frostburg State University, where she coordinated the multi-institutional research consortium, Appalachian Center for Ethnobotanical Studies. In 2023, Mimi received a Botanical Excellence Award from the American Botanical Council, a prestigious honor recognizing her as a Mark Blumenthal Herbal Community Builder. Mimi recently published her first book, the National Geographic Herbal.
