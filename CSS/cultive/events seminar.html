Dyslipidemia and Metabolic Dysfunction: An Integrative Approach to Inflammatory Syndromes

Saturday, November 7, 2026 8:00 AM - 2:00 PM MT

Non-CE

Denver Art Museum 100 W 14th Ave Pkwy Denver, CO 80204

This comprehensive course delves into the latest clinical protocols for managing physiological status and sufficiency of fat-soluble vitamins and explores the complex interplay of lipids, metabolism, and inflammation in patient health. Participants will gain insights into common physiological challenges, dyslipidemia, and metabolic syndromes, as well as the clinical importance of co-vitamins, physiological, and mineral catalysts. Through a combination of lecture, case studies, and practical applications, the course will equip healthcare providers with knowledge and tools to effectively address lipid disorders, inflammatory conditions, and autoimmune syndromes in their practice.
