Where Gut Health and Immune Support Come Together

Supplements Made for Growing Bodies

Children’s nutritional needs are unique—and they change as kids grow. From digestion and immune development to daily energy and resilience, foundational support plays an important role in overall health.

SP Children’s® products are developed with these needs in mind, using nutrient-dense, whole food-based ingredients and carefully selected compounds to support the body’s natural processes. Each formula is designed to fit into real family routines, making daily nutrition feel approachable and supportive.

Meet SP Children’s® ProSynbiotic

SP Children’s® ProSynbiotic is a 3-in-1 synbiotic designed to support healthy bowel movements, microbiome balance, and immune health in children. It brings together clinically researched probiotics, a supportive yeast, and naturally occurring fibers from whole food–based sources to work in harmony with the developing gut.*

The flavorless powder mixes easily into foods or drinks children already enjoy, making daily gut support simple—even for picky eaters.

Daily Immune Support, Made Kid-Friendly

SP Children’s® Immune contains prebiotic 2’-FL, bovine colostrum, and key nutrients like vitamin D and zinc, with flavor from organic elderberry and whole strawberry. It’s designed to complement daily routines and help support immune foundations during everyday challenges like school, travel, and seasonal changes.*

Together with SP Children’s® ProSynbiotic, it helps support two closely connected systems—gut health and immune function.
