Association Involvement

With a commitment to improving patient outcomes, we have always believed in the power of collaboration. Our involvement in key industry associations allows us to ensure that we are staying current on all of the critical topics and research. It also gives us the opportunity to share our learnings with the greater community in an effort to advance knowledge and patient care whenever possible.

Partnerships and Support

Association Support

Foundation for Chiropractic Progress

We are involved as corporate sponsors in the Campaign for Chiropractic. Our president, Charlie DuBois, is the chair of the Product and Service Suppliers Advisory Committee. Organized by the Foundation for Chiropractic Progress, the campaign objectives are:

  • To give chiropractic more positive media exposure
  • To motivate the general public to consider and ultimately utilize chiropractic services
  • To create an identity of professionalism, which is consistent with consumer expectations
  • To increase patient visits
  • To give the chiropractic practitioner the marketing support they need to build and maintain a successful practice. For more information on the Campaign for Chiropractic, visit http://www.f4cp.com/
