What Is The Microbiome?

The microbiome is a population of trillions of microbes living alongside human cells. It’s vital to whole-body health.

How Does The Gut Impact Immune Health?

The immune system protects against pathogens and toxins. A healthy gut helps regulate immune response and resilience.

What Are Prebiotics & Probiotics?

Probiotics are live microbes that support health. Prebiotics feed good bacteria in the gut to help them thrive.
