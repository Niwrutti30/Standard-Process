Why Do We Collect Your Information?

We collect information to provide you with the best shopping experience. This includes processing your orders, improving our website, and offering personalized recommendations. We also use your data to communicate with you about promotions, updates, and customer support.

Rest assured, we only collect the necessary information to make your experience smooth and secure. We respect your privacy and never sell your personal data to third parties.

What Information Do We Collect?

When you browse our website or make a purchase, we may collect certain details to process your order and improve our services. The information we may collect includes:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number to contact you about your orders.
  • Shipping and billing address to ensure correct delivery.
  • Payment details for processing transactions securely.
  • Order history and preferences to offer better recommendations.
  • Device and browser information to enhance website performance.

We only collect information that helps us serve you better. You can always review and update your details in your account settings.

How Do We Use Your Information?

Your data is used to improve your shopping experience and provide reliable customer service. We use the information to:

  • Process your orders and ensure timely delivery.
  • Send order confirmations, updates, and tracking information.
  • Provide customer support when you need help.
  • Recommend products based on your interests.
  • Improve our website and make it easier to use.

We value your trust and use security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

Is Your Information Secure?

Yes! We take your security seriously. Our website uses encryption and other security measures to keep your personal data safe. Payment information is processed through secure gateways to prevent fraud and unauthorized transactions.

We also limit access to your data. Only authorized staff members can view your information to assist you with orders and support requests.

Do We Share Your Information?

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share necessary details with trusted partners, such as shipping carriers and payment providers, to complete your transactions.

For example, we share your address with delivery services to ship your order. We also work with secure payment providers to process transactions safely. These partners follow strict privacy policies to protect your data.

Your Privacy Rights

You have full control over your personal information. You can:

  • Review and update your details in your account settings.
  • Request to delete your personal data if you no longer wish to keep an account with us.
  • Opt-out of marketing emails if you do not want to receive promotions.

We respect your choices and make it easy for you to manage your data.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and tracking technologies to improve your shopping experience. These small data files help us remember your preferences and show you relevant products.

You can manage your cookie settings anytime in your browser. Disabling cookies may affect certain website features, but we ensure that your shopping experience remains smooth.
