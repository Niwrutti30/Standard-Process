Recognizing Secure Websites

When shopping online, always look for security indicators:

  • A padlock icon in the address bar, indicating a secure connection.
  • A website address that starts with "https://" (the "s" stands for secure).
  • Official payment providers, such as PayPal or major credit card processors.

Account Security Tips

To keep your account secure, follow these simple tips:

  • Use a strong password with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Never share your password or payment details with anyone.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) if available.
  • Always log out of your account when using a shared device.
