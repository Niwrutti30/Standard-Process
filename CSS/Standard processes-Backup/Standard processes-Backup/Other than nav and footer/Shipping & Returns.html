We want to make shipping and returns as simple and hassle-free as possible. Below, you will find all the details about how we handle shipping, delivery times, and returns.

Shipping Information

We offer fast and reliable shipping to ensure your orders reach you on time. Once your order is placed, our team works quickly to process and ship your items.

Shipping Methods

We provide several shipping options to suit your needs:

  • Standard Shipping – Delivered within 5-7 business days.
  • Express Shipping – Delivered within 2-3 business days.
  • Overnight Shipping – Delivered the next business day.

Delivery times may vary based on your location and selected shipping method.

Order Processing Time

We process orders Monday through Friday. Orders placed before 12 PM (EST) are processed the same day. Orders placed after 12 PM (EST) or on weekends will be processed the next business day.

Custom or bulk orders may require additional processing time.

International Shipping

We ship worldwide! International shipping times vary depending on the destination and customs processing. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your shipment on the carrier’s website.

If you do not receive tracking details within the expected timeframe, please contact our support team.

Returns & Refunds

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with your order, you can return it within our return policy period.

Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, items must be in their original condition and packaging. Some products, such as personalized or hygiene-related items, may not be eligible for returns.

How to Return an Item

  1. Check our return policy to ensure your item is eligible for return.
  2. Contact our customer service team to request a return authorization.
  3. Pack your item securely and include any necessary documents.
  4. Ship the package using a trackable shipping method.
  5. Once we receive your return, we will process your refund or exchange.

Refund Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. The refund will be issued to your original payment method.

If you do not see your refund within this period, please check with your bank or payment provider.

Exchanges

If you need a different size or color, we offer exchanges for eligible items. Contact us to arrange an exchange, and we will guide you through the process.
