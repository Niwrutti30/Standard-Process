Accessibility Statement

Standard Process is always working to ensure that our website and services are accessible to everyone. If you are having difficulty accessing our website or you have any comments regarding our website, suggestions regarding techniques we might employ to make your experience easier, or concerns about issues we may have missed, please email us at sporders@standardprocess.com or call 800-558-8740. In your communication to us, please specify the nature of the accessibility issue you are raising, including the web address that may have presented an accessibility challenge. If you are in need of accessibility assistance, we will work with you to provide the information, item, or transaction you seek through an alternate communication method or medium that is accessible for you consistent with applicable law (for example, through telephone support).
