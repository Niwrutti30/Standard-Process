Sales Tax - Resale and Exemption Certificate Information

For tax questions related to your practice, we strongly encourage you to speak with a professional, as we are unable to provide tax advice. As a supplier, we are required to charge and collect sales tax (based on delivery address) on all orders unless we have a valid sales tax exemption certificate on file.

To submit a sales tax exemption certificate online you must be logged into your Standard Process account. Navigate to Profile -> Information -> Manage Tax Forms (ECW) and follow the prompts. Forms can also be emailed to taxinfo@standardprocess.com or fax: 800-438-3799. Include your Standard Process account number in all correspondence. Please note the approval process may take up to 2 business days.
