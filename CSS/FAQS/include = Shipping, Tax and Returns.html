UPS Ground Saver is available for $7 on patient orders under $150. Patient orders of $150 or more ship free. Patients can choose expedited shipping during checkout for an additional charge.

UPS Ground Saver is not available for orders shipping to Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, Hawaii, PO Boxes, or APO addresses. Orders under $150 (patient price) shipping to these locations will use UPS Ground for $12. Orders totaling $150 or more ship free.

Patient orders are typically shipped from a Standard Process facility within one to three business days.

Patients can check their shipping confirmation email for tracking information. They can also log in to the Standard Process website, open the Profile menu, then select My Orders to view order details and tracking information.

Patients can view and reorder previous purchases by logging in to the Standard Process website, opening the Profile menu, and selecting My Orders.

Health care professionals are copied on each patient order confirmation email. To view patient orders online, log in to the Standard Process website, open the Profile menu, then select My Profile > Patient Direct by Standard Process > Patient Orders.

Sales tax is calculated based on the patient’s delivery address and collected when required by state law. Tax may apply to products and shipping charges, depending on the state. Standard Process collects and submits the applicable sales tax on your behalf.

Health care professionals can view Patient Direct sales tax information on the Remuneration Report page. Log in to the Standard Process website, open the Profile menu, then select Practitioner Programs > Remuneration Report.

Patient Direct provides access to all Standard Process and MediHerb products. You may choose to remove any products that you do not wish to offer to your patients.

You can create personalized product recommendations for your patients using Patient Direct Protocols.

  • Log in to the Standard Process website, open the Profile menu, then select Practitioner Programs > Patient Direct > Manage Protocols.
  • Click Create Protocol.
  • Enter a protocol name and, if desired, add a description or notes.
  • Add products by entering the item name or product number, then enter the desired quantity.
  • Click Create.
  • Log in to the Standard Process website, open the Profile menu, then select Practitioner Programs > Patient Direct > Manage Patients.
  • Locate the patient in the Patient List, then select Send Protocol.
  • Select the protocol name from the list and add notes as needed.
  • Click Send.
Is Patient Direct just for my existing patients?

Patient Direct is available to both your existing patients and new patients who request to connect with your practice.

How can patients connect with my practice through Patient Direct?

Patients can connect with your practice in several ways:

  • With your Patient Direct code: Patients enter the code you provide to connect directly with your practice.
  • By invitation: You can invite patients to join your practice through Patient Direct.
  • By request: Patients can search the Standard Process website and select Become a Patient to request a connection with your practice.
How do patients use my code to register for Patient Direct?
  • After your consultation, provide your patient with your Patient Direct code and direct them to www.standardprocess.com.
  • From the quick links at the bottom of the page, patients can select Enter Patient Direct Code.
  • Patients will be prompted to create an account or log in to their existing account.
  • Once logged in, patients can enter your Patient Direct code or your practice name, then select Proceed to Agreements to continue with registration.
How do I invite patients?
  • Log in to the Standard Process website, open the Profile menu, then select Practitioner Programs > Patient Direct > Manage Patients.
  • In the Patient Invitation List, click Invite Patient.
  • Enter the patient’s email address, first name, and last name.
  • Click Send Invitation.

Your patient will receive an email invitation. After selecting Accept Invitation, they will be directed to the Standard Process website to log in or create a consumer account and connect with your practice through Patient Direct.

How do my patients request access to Patient Direct?

Patients can request access on the Standard Process website from several places, including:

  • Where to Buy/Health Care Professional Referral Page: Search by ZIP code and select Become a Patient.
  • Product Detail Page: Select Find a Participating Practitioner, then click Become a Patient.
  • My Profile Page: Select Apply for Patient Direct, then enter a practice name, Patient Direct code, or ZIP code to find a practice location.
I see patients at multiple practice locations. Can I list more than one location on the Where to Buy/Health Care Professional Referral Page?

Yes. Log in to the Standard Process website, open the Profile menu, then select Practice Locations. Select the Create Location button, enter the location address and contact details, then click Save.

How will I know if a patient has requested access?

You will receive an email notification when a patient requests access. You must approve your patient before they can place orders through Patient Direct. The notification email will include a link where you can activate the patient and suggest a protocol.

Why do I have to approve each patient before the patient can order products?

Patient Direct was designed to provide convenient 24/7 ordering while maintaining the practitioner-patient relationship. Approval connects patients with your practice and gives them access to the products and protocols you recommend for them.

How do I approve my patients?

Log in to the Standard Process website, open the Profile menu, then select Practitioner Programs > Patient Direct > Manage Patients. Find the patient in your Patient List, then select Approve or Deny as needed.

Can patients connect with more than one health care professional?

Patients can connect with more than one health care professional or clinic. They can view all approved connections by logging in to the Standard Process website, opening the Profile menu, and selecting My Practitioner. When starting an order, the patient chooses the health care professional or clinic associated with the purchase. Available products, pricing, protocols, recommendations, and remuneration are based on the connection selected for that order.

Can patients combine shopping carts connected to different practices?

Patients should select the health care professional or clinic they want associated with the purchase before adding products to their cart. Carts connected to different practices cannot be combined, and completed orders remain associated with the practice selected for that order.
