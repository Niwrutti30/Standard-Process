What is the list price on products through Patient Direct?

The default Patient Direct list price is 20% above the pricing shown on the current Standard Process Order Form.

How is sales tax calculated on Patient Direct orders?

Sales tax is calculated based on the patient’s delivery address and collected when required by state law. Standard Process collects and submits applicable sales tax on behalf of the practitioner.

Can I offer a discount to my patients?

Yes. Log in to the Standard Process website and open the Profile Menu. Select Practitioner Programs, Patient Direct, then Patient Direct Pricing to choose a pricing option.

What pricing options are available?

Practitioners can choose from Patient Direct price levels of 120% SLP with 45% remuneration, 110% SLP with 40% remuneration, or 100% SLP with 34% remuneration.

Can I offer AutoShip & Save?

Practitioners may choose to offer a 10% AutoShip & Save discount to patients enrolled in AutoShip.

How does the AutoShip & Save discount affect remuneration?

The AutoShip & Save discount is shared between Standard Process and the health care professional. Standard Process covers 60% of the discount, and the health care professional covers 40%.

Will existing AutoShip patients automatically receive the AutoShip & Save discount?

If AutoShip & Save is enabled, existing AutoShip patients must cancel their current AutoShip items and create a new subscription to receive the discount on future recurring orders.
