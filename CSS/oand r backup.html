Managing your orders and returns is easy with our store. We want to make sure you are happy with your purchase. Below, you will find all the information you need about placing orders, tracking shipments, and returning products if needed.

Placing an Order

Ordering from our store is simple and secure. Just browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Once you place your order, you will receive a confirmation email with the details.

If you need help while placing an order, our customer support team is available to assist you.

Order Processing Time

We process orders as quickly as possible. Standard processing times are:

  • Orders placed before 12 PM (EST) are processed the same day.
  • Orders placed after 12 PM (EST) or on weekends are processed the next business day.
  • Custom or bulk orders may take additional time to prepare.

We will notify you if there are any delays.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your package on the carrier’s website.

If you do not receive tracking details within the expected timeframe, please contact our support team.

Returns & Refunds

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with your order, you can return it within our return policy period.

To return an item, make sure it is in its original condition and packaging. Some products may not be eligible for returns due to hygiene or customization reasons.

How to Start a Return

  1. Check our return policy to ensure your item is eligible for a return.
  2. Contact our customer service team to request a return authorization.
  3. Pack your item securely and include any necessary documents.
  4. Ship the package using a trackable shipping method.
  5. Once we receive your return, we will process your refund or exchange.

Refund Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. The refund will be issued to your original payment method.

If you do not see your refund within this period, please check with your bank or payment provider.

Exchanges

If you need a different size or color, we offer exchanges for eligible items. Contact us to arrange an exchange, and we will guide you through the process.
