Regardless of the type of stress, our bodies react with the same “fight-or-flight” response. When this happens, hormones are released – and they do not return to normal levels until the stress passes. If the stress does not pass, it can affect:2


  • the digestive system
  • metabolism
  • the cardiovascular system
  • the immune system
  • the integumentary system
  • the musculoskeletal system
  • healthy sleep
  • emotional balance

One of the most common side effects of occasional stress is unhealthy sleep habits. Occasional stress keeps you from entering the deeper sleep stages in which the body repairs and grows tissue and supports a healthy immune system. The REM sleep stage in particular helps with healthy mood regulation and memory.2

Neglecting healthy sleep affects the whole body, especially the immune system and metabolism. However, a lack of sleep can be a stressor itself and can impact our mental health. It can become a vicious cycle if left unchecked.

The Importance Of Nutrition To Optimize Sleep

Stress places a greater demand on the body for oxygen, energy, and nutrients. Supporting the body with the right nutrition can not only help with occasional stress but also support healthy sleep and mood.

A balanced diet can support a healthy immune system and the repair of damaged cells. It provides the extra energy needed to cope with stressful events. When diet alone isn’t giving you what you need, supplements like Drenamin and Ashwagandha Forte can be an easy way to get those nutrients into your body to help support occasional stress and mood.
