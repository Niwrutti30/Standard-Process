Employee Benefits

It means a lot to us that our employees consider us a top workplace. In fact, many employees have grown their careers here for ten, twenty, and even forty years. At the same time, we’ve been fortunate that our success has led to significant growth, allowing us to continue bringing in talent and ideas. It is not uncommon to hear people tell us, “your employees are so happy.” We work hard to keep it that way. We offer our employees and their families a comprehensive and competitive benefits package, which includes:

Top Workplace

We capture the voice of our employees and celebrate a culture worthy of recognition as a Top Workplace USA 2024 and a Top Workplace in the Southeast Wisconsin region in 2021-2024!

Company Events

Company-hosted outings and events offer opportunities for us to relax and have some fun with co-workers and family.

Educational Assistance

We encourage and support continuing education that aligns with career paths, including partial reimbursement of tuition.

Time Off

We believe we do our best work when we are refreshed and rested. We offer generous paid vacation and holiday time for all employees.

Childcare

You can enjoy the ease and peace-of-mind of our on-site childcare, VitaKids, which offers a highly accredited curriculum through Bright Horizons.

Profit Sharing

Our success comes from working together. As a result of these efforts, Standard Process awards employees a percentage of the company’s profits through our profit sharing program.

401K

Standard Process offers employees a generous company matched 401k plan.

Health Benefits

Because health is our top priority, we offer comprehensive health care benefits, including dental, vision, and pet insurance.

Parental Leave

We offer a generous paid parental leave program.

Compensation

We offer competitive salaries and annual incentive programs to all our employees.

Community Events

Strong community involvement is important to us here at Standard Process. We are always looking for new ways to support our community.

Wellness Program

Staying healthy is easy with our Platinum WELCOA award-winning wellness program, including:

  • On-site 24x7 fitness center
  • Whole Food Court
  • On-site chiropractic care
  • On-site massage therapy
  • Fitness classes
  • Life coaching
  • $450 monthly Standard Process supplement allowance
  • Annual wellness incentives
  • Financial wellness programs

A Unique Culture of Family and Fun

As a family-owned company for three generations, our family values are very important to us and are evident throughout the entire organization. Employees here are seen as family – many have relatives working here. We work hard and truly enjoy each other, both inside and outside of work. At Standard Process, our goal is to see our employees succeed, so we provide the necessary tools and opportunities they need to excel, both personally and professionally.
