Collaborate With Us

Our collaboration program is designed for researchers who can help us improve our human and pet supplements.

Collaborative Studies

Interested, qualified parties will be invited to meet with the Standard Process clinic team to discuss collaborations and funding opportunities all year round. Together with the potential study Principal Investigator (PI), we will develop a study synopsis and comprehensive proposal. Please note that Standard Process caps indirect costs at no more than 15% for these studies.

If you are interested in collaborating with us, please contact us at: spclinic@standardprocess.com

Investigator-Initiated Studies

Standard Process is open to research ideas related to our products and research gaps for both human and veterinary applications. Below are the instructions for submitting documents for investigator-initiated studies:

  • Access and download the Letter of Intent (LOI).
  • Submit your LOI to spclinic@standardprocess.com for review. Reviews typically take 2-3 weeks.
  • If the proposal is accepted, the Standard Process clinic team will reach out to discuss next steps.

Young Investigators Research Award

Standard Process is committed to nurturing emerging talent through our Young Investigators Research Award. This award supports graduate students and post-doctoral fellows in generating preliminary research findings that will expand the scope of ongoing research projects.

  • Funding cycle begins annually on September 1 and concludes on October 30
  • Each cycle funds two deserving applicants
  • Recipients receive a one-year stipend of up to $35,000, which can be utilized for research supplies and travel expenses to present at national conferences
  • Indirect costs are capped at 10%

Applicants are required to submit their Letter of Intent (LOI) at the start of the application cycle. Applications must include the name of their major advisor or Principal Investigator overseeing the project. Human and veterinary research topics are eligible.

To apply, submit your LOI to spclinic@standardprocess.com.
