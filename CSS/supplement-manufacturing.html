Maximized Nutrition. Minimal Processing.

It’s Our Way of Delivering Nutrient-Dense Ingredients

Standard Process is committed to staying at the forefront of technological innovation with our farming and manufacturing practices to improve the efficiency of production and the efficacy of our products. That’s why we utilize Refractance Window Drying (RWD) technology: a gentle processing technique that’s used to protect the unique nutrition, vibrant colors, and natural aromas of heat-sensitive ingredients — including those that are grown on the Standard Process certified organic farm. It’s one more way we deliver nutrition the way nature intended.

  • RWD protects delicate plant compounds, vibrant colors, and valuable nutrients

  • Preserves antioxidants and phytonutrients for superior whole food nutrition

  • Offers higher thermal efficiency compared to other methods

  • Increases efficiency and quality by decreasing drying time and energy consumption compared to legacy technology

RWD can help preserve nutrients and phytonutrients such as:

  • Folate

  • Chlorophyll

  • Beta-carotene

  • Quercetin

Isolated Production

We use isolated suites in all of the following processes to enhance cleanliness and reduce the risk of cross-contamination in the manufacturing area

Mixing

Standard Process employees carefully measure and mix raw materials. Because of the variability in natural ingredients, color may vary from batch to batch. No artificial colors are ever used and the basic formula remains the same.

Binding and Processing Aids

After mixing, our fluid bed granulator is used to add natural binding ingredients, such as honey and arabic gum, to the product batch for tableting. No artificial flavoring is ever used.

Tableting, Capsuling, and Powders

Our supplements are offered in several forms of delivery. The most common are tablets, capsules, softgels, and powders.

Finished Products

Our employees and processes allow for high volume production without sacrificing quality control. All of our finished products are routinely tested to ensure product safety and that label claims are met.

Packaging

Our products are packaged in bottles that protect the whole food-based nutrients from damaging light. Bottles are sealed and neck banded to guard against product tampering. The bottled product is then placed in a cardboard carton to further protect from light and provides for easy storage, protection, and shipping.

Distribution and Shipping

Standard Process has invested in multiple distribution centers throughout the United States that allow for two-day, or less, delivery to approximately 98% of our customers. Quality Assurance

We use methods that include high-performance thin-layer chromatography (HPTLC) to identify incoming raw materials and in-process materials and qualify them based on consistency, potency, and purity. HPTLC is used to create a unique banding pattern, or “fingerprint,” by isolating key chemical constituents to aid in identification of these materials.

View Our Process

Testing

Our microbiology lab averages approximately 900 assays per week, and our chemistry lab averages approximately 400 assays per week. We use analytical methods approved by nationally recognized evaluative bodies, including the Association of Analytical Communities (AOAC) and United States Pharmacopeia (USP).

Quality Audits

We work very closely with our suppliers, using a questionnaire for qualification and an auditing program designed to ensure quality. Planned site visits are conducted 12-14 times per year based on risk analysis. In addition, our purchasing department evaluates on-time delivery, customer service, invoicing, and more.

Sanitation

We believe there are no shortcuts when it comes to quality and safety, and we keep our facility in pristine condition. In between product runs, our equipment is taken apart, cleaned, and sanitized. Adenosine triphosphate (ATP) testing is used to validate the effectiveness of our cleaning process. Then, we clean the surrounding production suite, including floors, walls, and vent covers. This is all in addition to our routine monitoring.

Environmental Monitoring

Environmental Monitoring

In order to monitor the general level of manufacturing hygiene for the facility, Standard Process has implemented an environmental monitoring program. The program is twofold: it highlights the presence of any foodborne pathogen that may be in the facility, and it determines the source of these pathogens. Routine meetings between production and quality control personnel are held to maintain the highest level of cleanliness throughout all areas of the facility.
