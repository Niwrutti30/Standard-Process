Website Configuration

Manage Your Website Configuration

By creating a personalized online storefront, you're making it easier than ever for patients to access the nutritional products you offer while strengthening their connection to your practice.

Your inSITE store is an extension of your brand and an opportunity to enhance the patient experience beyond the office visit. Taking a few minutes to customize your store can help create a professional, seamless experience that reflects your practice and builds confidence with your patients.

To set yourself up for success, simply follow the step-by-step instructions on this page. About Us Configuration

Manage Your About Us Page

Your About Us page is your opportunity to introduce yourself, share your story, and help patients get to know the people behind the care. It's often one of the first places visitors go when deciding whether a practice is the right fit for them, making it a valuable tool for building trust and creating meaningful connections.

By adding a few personal touches, such as photos, your practice story, and information about your approach to wellness, you can create a warm and welcoming experience that reflects who you are and what makes your practice special.

Follow the steps below to bring your About Us page to life and create a lasting first impression for current and future patients.

1. Enable – Why How

Enabling your About Us section will allow your inSITE to be indexed by Google. This makes it possible for the general public to find your inSITE through a local search (e.g., "buy Standard Process supplements near me"). Toggle the switch under "Enable About Us" and fill in the rest of the information to activate this section.

2. Hero image Where, How, and Why

The hero image appears at the top of your About Us page. It is recommended that this image involves or represents your clinic or practitioner(s). Click "Select An Image" to upload a JPG or PNG file, then add a text description of the image in the corresponding "Alt Text" field.

3. Your Practitioner Photo Where, How, and Why

The practitioner photo appears below the hero image. It is recommended to include a photo of the practitioner here. Click "Select An Image" to upload a JPG or PNG file, then add a text description of the image in the corresponding "Alt Text" field.

inSITE Orders

Managing Your inSITE Orders

As your inSITE store grows, having easy access to order information can help you stay connected to your patients and deliver an exceptional experience beyond the clinic visit. Whether you're checking on a recent purchase, reviewing store activity, or helping a patient track an order, the inSITE order management tools are designed to give you quick access to the information you need.

Monitoring your orders can also provide valuable insights into patient engagement and the ongoing impact of your nutritional recommendations. With simple filtering options, downloadable reports, and built-in tracking details, you can efficiently manage your store while continuing to focus on what matters most supporting your patients' health and wellness goals.

The tools outlined make it easy to stay informed, answer patient questions with confidence, and keep your inSITE store running smoothly as your practice continues to grow. Follow the steps below to get the most out of your order management experience.

1. Filter

You may view your inSITE orders by clicking inSITE Orders under Practitioner Programs > inSITE. Click the Filter button to display the available filters to narrow down your search. Available filters include customer name, order number, order status, and date.

2. Download inSITE orders

To download these orders to a CSV file for use in programs like Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets, click the Download To CSV button in the upper right corner.

3. Get Tracking for consumers

To get the tracking information for an order, click the View button on the order’s row. Tracking information can be seen in the Tracking section of the order detail page.
