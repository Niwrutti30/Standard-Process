Sessions

From Menarche to Menopause: Clinical Strategies for Lifelong Women’s Health

Whole food-based nutrition, targeted micronutrient support, herbal therapies, and lifestyle interventions play a critical role in women's health across the lifespan. From preconception and pregnancy to perimenopause and beyond, these approaches work together to support hormonal health, metabolic function, and overall vitality. Health professionals who embrace these tools are uniquely positioned to provide meaningful, personalized care that helps women thrive through every season of life.

Mindy Pelz , DC

Dr. Mindy Pelz is a 2x New York Times bestselling author and a leading voicein women’s health, hormones, and aging. Her books, including Eat Like a Girl, Fast Like a Girl, and Age Like a Girl empower women to work with their biology, not against it. Through her top-ranked podcast, the Live Like a Girl Podcast, and YouTube channel with 110M+ views, Dr. Mindy is changing the menopause conversation, helping women unlock energy, brain clarity, and purpose in their second half of life.

Ellie Anderson , MHS, RDN, LD

Ellie combines whole-food nutrition, lifestyle medicine, functional lab interpretation, and therapeutic strategies to help practitioners and patients restore foundational health and achieve lasting wellness.

Jessica Dietrich-Marsh , DC
