Atlanta Marriott Northwest at Galleria

200 Interstate North Parkway SE, Atlanta, GA 30339

Here are a few convenient options for travel between Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport and the Renaissance:

Shared-Ride Shuttles

Please note that the hotel does not provide a complimentary airport shuttle. Guests who are interested in shared-ride shuttle service can make reservations through the Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport Ground Transportation page: ATL Ground Transportation.

All shared-ride shuttles pick up on the Shared-Ride Shuttle Aisle B, which is outside in the New Ground Transportation Center, located on the west end of the Domestic Terminal (Map: Shared-Ride Location). Specific pickup instructions will be provided at the time of booking based on the transportation provider selected.

Taxi / Rideshare (Uber/Lyft):

Taxis and rideshares are available at PHX 24/7. Travel time to the hotel is typically about 30 minutes, but fares can be higher than shuttle services depending on demand and traffic.

Renaissance Phoenix Downtown Hotel

100 North 1st St., Phoenix, AZ 85004

Here are a few convenient options for travel between Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport and the Renaissance:

Valley Metro Rail

Guests can take the free, 24/7 PHX Sky Train from their terminal to the 44th St/Washington Light Rail Station. From there, board the westbound Valley Metro Rail and travel into downtown Phoenix, exiting at the 3rd Street/Washington Station. The Renaissance Phoenix Downtown Hotel is just a short walk away. From the station, walk two blocks west on Washington Street, then turn right onto 1st Street to reach the hotel entrance. Full fare for a single ride is $2, or $4 for an all-day pass.

Taxi / Rideshare (Uber/Lyft):

Taxis and rideshares are available at PHX 24/7. Travel time to downtown is typically about 10-15 minutes, but fares can be higher than shuttle services depending on demand and traffic.
