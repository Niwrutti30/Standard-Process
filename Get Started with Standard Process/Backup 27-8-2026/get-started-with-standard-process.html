Step 1: Sign In to Your Account

Sign In to Your Account

We've made signing in simpler. Returning users please note, your previous username is no longer used. Instead, you'll sign in with the email address associated to your account. For safety reasons you'll need to reset your password the first time you log in to the new Standard Process website. Don't remember which email you used? Simply use the Forgot Email tool to locate your account, then reset your password and you'll be ready to sign in.

New to Standard Process?

Create an account here or check out as a guest whenever you're ready.

Returning to Standard Process?

To keep your account secure, you'll need to reset your password the first time you sign in.

1. Visit the Log In page.

2. Select Forgot Password .

3. Enter your email address and select Reset Password .

4. Check your inbox and follow the instructions to create a new password. If you'd like, you can reuse your previous password.

5. Sign in using your email address and your new password.

Don't Remember Your Email Address?

No problem, we can help you find it.

1. Select Forgot Email? from the Log In page.

2. Enter your previous username.

3. We'll send you an email with the address associated with your account.

4. Return to the Log In page and reset your password using that email address.

Step 2: Manage Your Account

Manage Your Account

Everything you need to manage your Standard Process experience is available in one convenient place. Your account makes it easy to update your information, keep track of orders, reconnect with your Healthcare Practitioners, and quickly access the products and recommendations that support your wellness journey.

Practitioner Store Selector

If you've shopped with multiple Healthcare Practitioners, switching between their stores is easy. Use the Current Practitioner Store selector in the website header to move between practitioner storefronts, giving you convenient access to the products, recommendations, and ordering experience associated with each provider.


From Your Dashboard You Can

  • Update your addresses and saved payment methods.

  • Manage your communication preferences.

  • View current and past orders.

  • Reorder products you've purchased previously.

  • Quickly access your favorite products.

  • View Healthcare Practitioners you've shopped with or connected with through Patient Direct.

  • Enroll in Patient Direct to receive personalized recommendations from your Healthcare Practitioner.

Step 3: Start Shopping

Relationships Matter

Your account remembers the Healthcare Practitioners you've ordered from, making it easy to continue shopping with the provider you trust while giving you the flexibility to discover new practitioners whenever you choose. Upon login you can select to shop from a Healthcare Provided you previously order from by selecting their store or find a new Practitioner.

Start Shopping

At Standard Process, we believe the best health outcomes come from partnering with a Healthcare Practitioner. That's why our nutritional supplements are available through Healthcare Practitioners who can provide personalized recommendations and ongoing support based on your individual wellness goals.

Whether you're shopping with a trusted practitioner, looking to connect with one for the first time, or simply reordering products you've already been recommended, we've made the experience simple, convenient, and centered around the practitioner-patient relationship.


Ways to Get Started

  • Continue with a Practitioner You Trust – If you've ordered from a Healthcare Practitioner before, simply sign in and choose from your previously connected practitioners to continue shopping.

  • Order with a Healthcare Practitioner – Find the supplements you need and select Order Here from the product page to purchase through a participating Healthcare Practitioner.

  • Find a New Healthcare Practitioner – Use our Find a Practitioner tool to discover a participating Healthcare Practitioner near you and begin your wellness journey.

  • Become a Patient – Request to become a patient to receive personalized supplement recommendations, practitioner-created protocols, and ongoing nutritional guidance.
