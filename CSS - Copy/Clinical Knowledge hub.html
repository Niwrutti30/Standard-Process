Investing in You, Your Patients, and Your Practice

At Standard Process, we believe knowledge is more than power - it is empowerment that serves to create positive health outcomes and change lives. As such, we strive to bring you relevant educational tools, events, courses, and literature that support your continued learning and reinforce your work with patients. These practitioner resources provide credible, evidence-based education to promote your professional development and practice success. Our patient education materials can be seamlessly integrated into your clinic, allowing you to focus on delivering high-quality care while easing the burden on your team and enhancing the health literacy of those in your practice.

Explore Our Nutrition Innovation Center

Our Nutrition Innovation Center (NIC) was developed by Standard Process as a first-of-its-kind science, technology and research facility driven by our mission to enhance human health and well-being through whole-person clinical care, organic farming, and whole food nutritional therapies. It is the leading clinical research center fully dedicated to supporting the present and future needs of natural health care practitioners like you. Our scientists and clinicians focus on testing and validating outcomes in therapeutic whole food nutrition as well as product and ingredient-specific benefits that help produce optimal health outcomes.
