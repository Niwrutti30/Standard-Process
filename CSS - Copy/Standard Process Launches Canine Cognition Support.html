Standard Process® Launches Canine Cognition Support to Help Aging Dogs Maintain Brain Health

New Veterinary Formulas™ supplement supports alertness and awareness with clinically researched ingredients

2/24/2026

PALMYRA, WI — February 24, 2026 — Standard Process®, a pioneer in whole food-based nutritional supplements, today announced the launch of Canine Cognition Support – a calming supplement for dogs & cats – with ingredients shown to provide relief from occasional anxiousness and promote a positive mood and peace of mind for pets.

Everyday struggles like separation anxiety, or stressful situations like thunderstorms, fireworks, car rides, unfamiliar environments, grooming and veterinary visits, can lead to signs that your pet might need more support to manage their emotions and behaviors. This is why Spirited Paw created Calm – a supplement that promotes a calm demeanor, aides in relaxation, and helps your pet manage behavior in stressful situations.

Like all Spirited Paw products, Calm is made by Standard Process and contains science-backed ingredients, as well as plant-based nutrients grown on the 868-acre USDA Certified Organic Standard Process farm.

“These wafers are a great and effective supplement to help enhance your pet’s relaxation regimen,” says Katrina Verdeur, MS, Product Development Manager for Pet Products at Standard Process. “Starting with active doses of L-theanine, L-tryptophan, and magnesium, then adding chamomile extract, ginger, kalette, apple and liver powders, this product was designed to support calming with effective, friendly ingredients.”

Calm is made with researched ingredients like L-Theanine, L-Tryptophan, and Magnesium. L-Theanine has been clinically tested in both cats and dogs, and L-Tryptophan has been clinically tested in dogs; both show favorable results in promoting calm behavior. Marine-derived magnesium also helps to actively support a normal stress response.

Spirited Paw Calm also contains ginger and chamomile extract, as well as organic Kalette, which is grown at the Standard Process certified organic farm.

By harnessing the power of nature and science, each Spirited Paw formula is carefully crafted and rigorously tested to ensure superior safety and effectiveness to help pets live healthier, happier lives.

Spirited Paw Calm is now available for purchase at www.spiritedpaw.com
