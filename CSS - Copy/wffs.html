Who Qualifies For This Program?

This unique educational opportunity was designed to make it easier for Certified Traditional Naturopaths and Advanced Nutrition Health Coaches to qualify for a Standard Process Professional Account. Upon completion of this course, the following practitioners are eligible for a Professional Account.

Certified Traditional Naturopaths

Certified through a course completed at an accredited school:

  • American Naturopathic Certification Board (ANCB)
  • American Council for Holistic Medicine (ACHM)
  • American Naturopathic Medicine Certification Board (ANMCB)
  • American Association of Natural Wellness
  • Practitioners/American Natural Wellness Practitioners Board
  • International Association of Natural Health Practitioners (IANHP)

Board certified through one of the following accreditors:

  • Trinity School of Natural Health
  • Genesis School of Natural Health (AHG Member School)
  • New Eden School of Natural Health & Herbal Studies

Advanced Nutrition Health Coaches

Individuals who have successfully completed one of the following programs:

  • Functional Diagnostic Nutrition (FDN) Practitioner
  • ADAPT Health Coach
  • WellCoaches
  • AFPA Holistic Health Coach
  • Functional Nutrition Lab
  • Institute for Integrative Nutrition
  • Duke University Integrative Medicine Health Coach
  • Functional Medicine Coaching Academy (IFM)
  • BS in Health Coaching - Life University
  • National Society of Health Coaches
  • Dr. Sears Wellness Institute Health Coach
  • MUIH Post-Baccalaureate Certificate in Health and Wellness Coaching
