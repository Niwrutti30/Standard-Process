We are Obsessed With Soil Quality

Our family-owned 850+ acre certified organic farm provides ingredients you can trust. We grow 20 – 30 unique crops each year, generating over 7 million pounds of produce including buckwheat, Swiss chard and Spanish black radish.

Because plants can only be as healthy as the ground they are grown in, Standard Process is committed to sustainable and regenerative farming, improving the health of the soil season over season. This is in sharp contrast to most conventional farms that have seen a decline in soil quality year over year.

As a result, you can trust that the ingredients in our supplements will never contain GMOs or be subjected to synthetic pesticides. To ensure freshness and limit nutrient degradation, innovative processing techniques to preserve potency and nutrient density.

Go Further with Standard Process

Ever wonder about soil microbial diversity and its impact on the nutritional composition of foods? Or how whole foods support human health and physiological function? At Standard Process, we do. Questions like this inspired us to open the Nutrition Innovation Center or as we like to call it, the NIC. The Standard Process Nutrition Innovation Center opened in early 2018. Located in the North Carolina Research Center in Kannapolis, the NIC not only inspires innovation, but it also validates our whole food philosophy with scientifically based evidence. The NIC provides us with yet another way to continue the mission of our founder, Dr. Royal Lee, to combat the declining nutrition of the American diet, and to change lives through whole foods.

"The Nutrition Innovation Center is the catalyst for our new era of innovation. It is the leading and active clinical research center of its kind, fully dedicated to supporting the present and future needs of natural health care practitioners and their practices by focusing on clinical outcomes in therapeutic care as well as product-specific benefits and outcomes.”

— Charlie DuBois, President & CEO, Standard Process
