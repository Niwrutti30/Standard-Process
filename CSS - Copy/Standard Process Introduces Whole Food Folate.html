Standard Process Introduces Whole Food Folate™

The First Folate of Its Kind, Powered by Superfoods for Maximum Bioactivity

7/9/2025

PALMYRA, WI, July 9, 2025 — Standard Process®, a pioneer in whole food-based nutritional supplements for over 95 years, proudly announces the release of Whole Food Folate™ — a one-of-a-kind supplement that delivers vitamin B 12 and multiple forms of naturally occurring folate.

Crafted with organic collard greens and turnip greens grown on the Standard Process certified organic farm — along with golden chlorella, a superfood microalga — Whole Food Folate™ is free from folic acid and delivers multiple forms of naturally occurring folate. They include the physically active methyl folate and formyl folate — essential for critical functions such as DNA synthesis, methylation, cardiovascular health, and overall cellular health. These folate forms are also especially beneficial for individuals with genetic variations that impact folate metabolism.*

Whether due to dietary insufficiencies or challenges with folate conversion, Whole Food Folate™ is an ideal choice for those experiencing folate deficiency, facing folic acid buildup, or who prefer a natural alternative. Whole Food Folate™ also supports central nervous system function, red blood cell health, and cellular processes such as DNA formation and replication.*

“Our goal with Whole Food Folate is to provide a solution that taps into the power of nature, offering bioavailable nutrients that are gentle on the body and support long-term wellness,” says Bassem F. El-Khodor, PhD, — Director of Discovery Science and Innovation for Standard Process. “By using organic greens like collard and turnip greens, we deliver a whole food source of folate that does not require the body's activation process. This makes it a superior choice for individuals seeking naturally occurring folate to support cardiovascular, neurological health, and other benefits.”

As part of the Standard Process® family, Whole Food Folate™ represents the company’s ongoing commitment to whole food-based solutions, sustainable sourcing, and clinical excellence. The product is now available through health care professionals and authorized Standard Process retailers.
