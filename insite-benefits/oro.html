Frequently Asked Questions

Can I give Calming Comfort Pro to my dog or cat with other medications?

As it is designed to be used, the ingredients in Calming Comfort Pro have no known contraindications or interactions with any drug. It is always best to consult your veterinary professional when considering the addition of a supplement to your pets’ healthcare plan. Find a full list of available Standard Process suppliers here.

Is the product palatable?

When tested in our in-home trial, pet parents reported acceptance 86% of the time. The liver powder in the product aids in palatability and has been well accepted.

How long does a bottle last?

The length of time a bottle will last is based on the animal’s weight. There are 172 - 1/4 tsp servings in the bottle. A 1/4 tsp scoop is included in the bottle along with administration instructions by weight on the bottle label.

Is this product appropriate for pets with noise sensitivities?

Yes. The ingredients in Calming Comfort Pro work synergistically to provide comforting and calming support to cats and dogs including those with noise sensitivities.

What makes this product different from others on the market?

Calming Comfort Pro was formulated by the nutrition scientists at our Nutrition Innovation Center. Adhering to our philosophy of providing nutrients as nature intended, Calming Comfort Pro is a whole food-based option for a multimodal approach to supporting animals experiencing fear, stress, and anxiousness.

How long does this calming supplement take to work?

This supplement supports relaxation and works best when given daily for at least 30 days. Some pets may show results sooner, but consistency is key. Regular use will also help cats and dogs stay calmer during stressful situations.
